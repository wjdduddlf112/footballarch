## Read before using any player grade

- Competition main-stage results and standings have been constructed from the 2013-14 source data and 362/475-goal season-level totals reconciled to UEFA. Data entry correctness and historical athlete correctness are distinct.
- Nominal 11-player club starting groups are reconstructed from 2013-14 historical club rosters, official final lineup cases, or representative knockout match records. They are NOT automatically certified as the most frequent starting XI across the entire tournament.
- UEFA 2014 technical-study-group best 18, top goals, assists and selected appearance statistics support a **restricted provisional grade subset**. Grades are editorial S–D hypotheses, NOT copies of UEFA awards. Previous two years' individual match-by-match research is not independently complete.
- If a player has `tier:null` or `rank:null`, the athlete is not assigned low status: **evidence_pending** is NOT tier D. Rank values apply only **within graded partial position pools**, not across all nominated players.
- The website's two 4-3-3/4-2-3-1 Best XI tables are unapproved hypotheses and do not establish definitive seasonal individual ranking. Match-source and 3-season checks remain pending.
