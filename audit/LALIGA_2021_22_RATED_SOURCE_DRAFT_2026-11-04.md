## B. Assessment design and tactical review
- **Formation shown: 4-4-2, PROVISIONAL.** 2019-20–2021-22 La Liga/Segunda formation research finds S4-4-2 among the most used, but does not establish a 2021-22 La Liga standalone modal frequency in the public abstract. Do NOT claim exact 2021-22 one-season mode independently verified. Research: https://ekoizpen-zientifikoa.ehu.eus/documentos/6834a8a6ff7c406929ad8af4 . The separate 2012-2021 La Liga study excludes this season and must not be used as 2021-22 sole-season proof.
- Team formations differ from representative season XI: Madrid/Barcelona 4-3-3, Betis/Sociedad often 4-2-3-1, Atleti 3-5-2 and wingbacks, Sevilla 4-3-3 variants, Villarreal several Emery 4-4-2/4-3-3 structures. Team tactical mapping must be independently match-audited.
- Source of 2021-22 league PM=appearances, SM=starts, G=goals: each actual BDFutbol first-division team season page. Source is manually transcribed here; independently reproducing all 131 league stats from a second statistical provider remains open.
- At least one league appearance required and 11 nominal tactical starters per eligible team. Caps GK 1+1, CB 2+1, remaining 1+1 except 2CM 2+1, two-ST 2+1. Unfilled alternates allowed, e.g. Atleti zero-game reserve GK excluded. One player = one primary role. L/R kept separate.
- **Three-year qualitative weighting 2021-22 highest > 2020-21 > 2019-20**, conceptually most recent 60%, previous 30%, earliest 10%. Previous two seasons are unverified DRAFT pointers for matching names, *not* an independent player-form re-evaluation. Do not treat this as a numerically calculated rating.
- Team trophies and final standings do not directly add tier points; current personal impact, continuity, role, starts and goals most important.
- World Class = S/A+; League Top Class = S/A+/A0/A-. Stored historical website compatible A/B/C appear as A0/B0/C0 here.
