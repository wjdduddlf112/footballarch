## B. Historical formation and method
- **League 2020-21 4-4-2**, backed by 2012-2021 peer-reviewed La Liga tactical study (4-4-2 most-used for 2017/18–2020/21): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10407311/ . Exact 2020-21 380-match position-by-position formation frequency was NOT reproduced here.
- Actual club formations differ: Atlético 3-5-2 and 4-4-2, Barcelona changed from 4-3-3 to 3-5-2, Real Madrid 4-3-3, Sevilla 4-3-3/4-2-3-1, Betis and Real Sociedad 4-2-3-1, Villarreal 4-3-3/4-2-3-1. Differences and positional proxies explicitly noted.
- Candidate shortlist first compiled from BDFutbol 2020-21 individual club-team PM (league appearances), SM (league starting appearances), G (goals). These are current-season single-source transcriptions, not independently double-checked. GK G on BDFutbol is goals conceded; this field is **not** recorded as keeper goals.
- Team player counts are based on **maximum** caps, not target quotas: GK2/CB3/LB2/RB2/DM2/CM3/CAM2/LW2/RW2/ST3; 11 nominal tactical starter roles per club. Multiple positions per person prohibited. Low-start backups sometimes omitted; Villarreal's Rulli only 2 league starts but also 2020-21 Europa final starting GK (candidate status contingent on cup context).
- Three-season weighting priority: **2020-21 > 2019-20 > 2018-19**, conceptual 60/30/10. The tiers are subjective qualitative proposals. Independent individual form research over previous two seasons remains open; historical draft values are not independently verified evidence, and foreign/prior nonqualifying clubs may be missing.
- Team league finishes are used for qualifying-club eligibility, **not** automatic player rating bonuses. Individual consistency, impact, playing time, competition with same-position contemporaries preferred.
- Tier display: S, A+, A0, A-, B+, B0, B-, C+, C0, C-; JSON storage uses existing normalized A/B/C. S/A+ World Class; S/A+/A0/A- League Top Class.
