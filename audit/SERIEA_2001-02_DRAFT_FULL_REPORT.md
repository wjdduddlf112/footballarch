## 1. Season and methodology

- Domestic evaluation: 2001-02 Serie A; 18 clubs, 34 match regular season. Earlier personal seasons **1999-00 (lowest weight), 2000-01 (middle), 2001-02 (highest)**. Club rank/Scudetto/Coppa Italia not directly credited to the individual's tier.
- Eligible team: appeared in 2001-02 Serie A **and** actually played a 2002-03 UEFA Champions League, UEFA Cup, or 2002 UEFA Intertoto match. Do not infer participation solely from league rank.
- Initial 137 historic candidate records were unapproved and not used as authoritative new selection; 16 missing researched, with one of the 16 (Gamberini) excluded due to position cap, and one old seed (Torino Garzya) also removed, leaving **151** nominations. This is still not fully independent new nomination of every club.
- Positional order GK, CB, LB, RB, DM, CM, CAM, LW, RW, ST; period LM/RM/LWB/RWB original role retained separately and mapped into site slots; player has exactly one site position.
- Each club is assigned **eleven starter-equivalent formation slots**, and **≤1 backup per position**. In teams with period back three, there can be **3 starter CB + 1 backup**; backup describes alternate formation slot, not necessarily a weak player or all-season bench status.
- Tiers S, A+, A0, A-, B+, B0, B-, C+, C0, C-, D. Stored schema A0=A, B0=B, C0=C. World Class S/A+. League Top Class S/A+/A0/A-.
- Same-tier provisional rank uses 2001-02 player usage/season impact as tiebreak; without player-by-player comprehensive contemporary tactical and form comparison this is NOT a finalized historical ranking.
