# Football Archive — UEFA 2014-15 complete match archive and player grade review (DRAFT)

Archive date: 2026-11-13; applies only to **2014-15** season, using preceding **2012-13 / 2013-14** as context. **Status: draft_unapproved**.

## 0. Methodology, scope, and data quality

- UCL candidate pool: exactly 2014-15 main-stage round-of-16 clubs; UEL candidate pool: exactly eight quarter-finalists. Evaluate club contribution and individual impact, not a club's trophy bonus.
- Club lineups are **historical nominal representative XI hypotheses**, drawn from same-season club rosters and partial UEFA knockout/final sources, with up to one backup per position. They are **NOT VERIFIED to be each club's most frequent UEFA lineup or UEFA match-started roles**. Every player must ultimately be checked for cup-tied status, match participation, and dominant European competition position.
- S means irreplaceable individual impact; A+ league/competition-wide irreplaceability; A0 is league top class; A- near top class; B+ long-term first XI nearly top; B0 established starter; B- first XI with weak impact; C+ to D are lower grades. Current review has source-led grade hypotheses for only a subset, not full 455-player decisions.
- Assess 2014-15 first and weight 2013-14 higher than 2012-13. **Full match-by-match independent individual audit over all three seasons remains unfinished**, so provisional grades are NOT final. A tier of `null` denotes `evidence_pending`, **not D**.
- Scores exclude shootout penalty goals and use post-extra-time scores only where applicable. Specifically the third-party UEL 2015-02-26 Beşiktaş-Liverpool leg incorrectly reports 6-4 including penalties; recorded as **1-0 after extra time; penalties 5-4**. This restores UEFA 548 goals and separates shootout from normal match scoring.
- Player appearance counts, match-primary positions, incomplete birthdates and some historical roster names require additional independent research; past domestic league tier references are not imported into UEFA tiers.
- Rankings cover only the **source-linked rated subset** at each position and **are not complete positional rankings** of the full candidate pool.
- Both Best XIs are **proposed_unapproved**, not official achievements or user-accepted final selections.
