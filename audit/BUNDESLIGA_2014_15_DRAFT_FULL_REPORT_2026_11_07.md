### Cup berth exception — newly in force in 2015
DFB-Pokal winner Wolfsburg finished second and entered UCL. Unlike preceding rules, the **losing Cup finalist Dortmund did not automatically inherit** the Europa League Cup berth. The reallocated UEL group-stage spot went to sixth-placed Schalke and UEL third qualifying to seventh-placed Dortmund. Borussia Dortmund's contemporaneous official explanation: https://www.bvb.de/de/de/aktuelles/news/news.html/News/Uebersicht/Ueber-den-DFB-Pokal-oder-die-Liga-Borussias-Wege-nach-Europa.html .
