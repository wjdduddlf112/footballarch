# La Liga 2007-08 — full 154-player DRAFT report

All nominated players are in the exact club squad for the 2007-08 domestic season according to the BDFutbol lineup / substitute season tables. This is an evidence-led PROVISIONAL evaluation, not a fully independently verified three-season historical study.

## Coverage and limitations

- **8 clubs**, selected using *2008-09 actual* European participation (four UCL, three direct UEFA Cup, Deportivo actual Intertoto and subsequent UEFA Cup).
- **154 candidates**. Exactly 11 nominal starting slots per club, 66 backup roles. One player appears in one position.
- One actual source for current 2007-08 starts / apps / goals per club. Current-season individual line is transcribed from the sources, with no automated full 154 second-source comparison.
- Birthdates listed in all rows; independent second source still required for every candidate.
- Tiers/ranks DRAFT and NOT user approved; 2005-06 and 2006-07 individual quality review incomplete for candidates outside earlier qualified-club archive.
- BDFutbol season lineup tables are not authoritative match-by-match evidence of predominant left/right or CB/fullback deployment. Atlético and Deportivo roles especially subject to revision.
- **2007-08 Best XI and 4-4-2 are editorial proposals**, not user approved and not empirically proven league most-common formation.
- This report does NOT claim Node CLI run or live website browser QA.

## Methodology

Tier rating is qualitative 2007-08 > 2006-07 > 2005-06, favoring individual contributions and minimizing final league position bonus. Grades: S world elite irreplaceable; A+ league best hard to replace; A0 league top; A- near league top; B+ fixed high-level starter; B0 starter; B- underperforming starter; C+ useful limited contribution; C0 routine rotation; C- low-impact rotation. No fabricated aggregate grade metric.

**Important role challenges:**
- Atlético Antonio López side allocation RB is unconfirmed by per-game evidence (left-back may be dominant). Perea CB/RB similarly uncertain.
- Deportivo 2007-08 switched to a five-man backline under Lotina in the second half; Pablo Amo used as actual third CB in parts of the run. 4-2-3-1 is a selection-slot proxy and must not be mistaken as confirmed full-season modal system.
- Racing Pablo Álvarez registered only one appearance for Deportivo, then 18 league appearances / 10 starts on loan at Racing, and registered Racing RW to avoid duplicate league selection.
- Real Madrid Sneijder was 27 starts, ranked in tier A; backup role is just tactical 4-4-2 central cap, not a claim of a bench-only performer.
- Valencia Arizmendi was included over Angulo based on season starts (18 vs 9).
