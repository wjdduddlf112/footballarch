## 2. Tactical model and evaluation methodology
- **League model: 4-4-2**, based on 2012–2021 peer-reviewed La Liga tactical frequency analysis, which identifies 4-4-2 as the most used during 2017-18 to 2020-21: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10407311/ . The 2019-20 exact 380-match reconstruction has not been independently repeated.
- **Qualitative three-season emphasis**: 2019-20 highest, 2018-19 second, 2017-18 third (conceptually 60/30/10). Previous DRAFT statistics are pointers only; full independent quality checks remain OPEN. Do not invent a numeric player score.
- Individual player contribution, match participation, position-specific performance and continuity prioritized. Club final standing not added as a direct tier bonus.
- Candidate position is a **single representative current-year role**, not a verified match-by-match majority. Display 4-4-2 XI is independent from club shapes. Wing sides treated distinctly; starters exactly 11 per club.
- BDFutbol PM=appearances, SM=starts, G=outfield goals. For goalkeepers, negative G is goals conceded and was correctly normalized to zero *player goals*.
- S, A+ = World Class; S, A+, A, A- = League Top Class. Stored site-normalized `A/B/C` correspond to display `A0/B0/C0`.
