2. Independently determine **each club's actual season representative XI (11 players)** from **starts and minutes** across the entire tournament; the 176+1 one-match starters are only evidence of one game, *not* an entire campaign's best lineup.
3. Determine one backup at each existing position only if actually starter-equivalent, not by force-fitting 20+ players per club. Confirm dominant positions and left/right roles from match evidence.
4. Establish primary independent player birthday sources, missing UCL starts/minutes/assists, scoring in current vs previous club, penalties excluding shootout.
5. Complete recent 2000-01 and 2001-02 player form evaluation for each, weighting 2002-03 European matches most. Verify the 30 provisional ratings against all qualified rivals.
