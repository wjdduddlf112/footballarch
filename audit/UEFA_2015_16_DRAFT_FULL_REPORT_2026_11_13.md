## 0. Boundaries, material caveats and verification type

- **Tournament records:** full 2015-16 UCL main-stage 32 entrants, eight groups and 125 games; full 2015-16 UEL main-stage 48 group entrants + 8 UCL third-place transferees, 12 groups and 205 games. Knockout scores, draws, goals and group points were reconstructed from historical match sources. Official UEFA competition goals (UCL 347 / UEL 536) reconciled; however not every fixture independently checked against its official UEFA match report.
- **Player eligibility:** the 24 evaluated clubs are correctly defined by UCL last-16 and UEL quarter-final places. **Club first XIs are tactical nomination hypotheses, not verified season-most-frequent UEFA match lineups**. Some nominations rely on 2015-16 league rosters or one representative European lineup. Not every candidate has been individually cross-checked for actual UEFA main-stage minutes, cup-tie regulations, prior club, registration or primary-position frequency.
- **Player tiers:** the 18 UCL and 16 UEL source-linked provisional grade hypotheses use contemporaneous UEFA technical-observer squad recognition and interpret the player's 2015-16 performance, not an automatic award-issued grade. **Independent 2013-14 and 2014-15 individual-quality review remains incomplete.** All other candidates are `tier:null`, `review:evidence_pending`; absence of evidence must not be converted to D.
- **Positional rankings:** rated-subset-only ranks, not exhaustive 1-to-N ranking of all nominees. **No tiers are formally approved; Best XIs are `proposed_unapproved`.**
- **Score correction:** the third-party CSV includes penalty shootout scores as if they were match scores for some ties. UCL Atlético–PSV 0-0 (8-7 pens), UCL Real Madrid–Atlético final 1-1 (5-3 pens), and UEL Sevilla–Athletic 1-2 (5-4 pens) were corrected. The Bayern–Juventus UCL second leg is 4-2 after extra time. Penalty shootouts were excluded from official goals.
- **Confirmed starting GK corrections:** UCL Benfica Júlio César 7 UEFA starts vs Ederson 3; UCL Wolfsburg Diego Benaglio 8 vs Koen Casteels 2; UEL Dortmund Roman Weidenfeller 10 vs Roman Bürki 2 (FBref/UEFA technical report). Other individual minutes and starts remain `null` pending confirmation.
