## 2. Representative formation and three-season assessment

**4-2-3-1 verified as modal 2016-17 Premier League formation**. PL 4 June 2017 reports almost one-third of all team starting lineups and 13 managers primarily using it (4-3-3 next on six). 143 back-three starts versus 617 back-four; exact integer for 4-2-3-1 not published.
Formation research: https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/406186

Weighting: **2016-17 60% / 2015-16 30% / 2014-15 10%**. Applied qualitatively; independent complete prior two season records are NOT obtained.

Club-specific 3-4-3 Chelsea and 4-3-3 Liverpool/Manchester City are distinct from the league's modal 4-2-3-1. For Chelsea, CB three starting central defenders (David Luiz, Cahill, Azpilicueta); Alonso and Moses recorded as LB/RB surrogate *wingbacks*, respectively, with actual roles explicitly annotated. This team-specific 3CB handling makes the true 11-man shape representable with the 10-role archive taxonomy, but no independent match-position majority audit has been done.
