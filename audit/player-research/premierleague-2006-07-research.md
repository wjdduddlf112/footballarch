## B. Formation conclusion: 2006-07 league 4-4-2 proposal versus club variation

4-4-2 is still the league-era nominal mainstream, consistent with the contemporary PFA season Best XI shape, but **the project does not possess complete 380-match shape-frequency data**. This is a provisional representative selection, not a quantitatively proven league-wide modal formation. Chelsea shifted between diamond 4-4-2 and 4-3-3 (UEFA's official 2006/07 Technical Report), Liverpool between 4-4-2 and 4-4-1-1; Bolton is modelled as 4-2-3-1/4-3-3 for lineup roles. Individual primary positions are provisional unless supported by individual match tracking.
