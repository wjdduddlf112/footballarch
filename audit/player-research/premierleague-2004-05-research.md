## F. 3-season weighting, exceptional cases, limitations

- 2004-05 is the primary evaluation season; 2003-04 secondary; 2002-03 tertiary. Each player's structured previousSeasons and narrative assessmentRationale can be inspected in the season JSON.
- The presence of an old candidate in an earlier archive has **not** driven 2004-05 inclusion. Old 2002-03 / 2003-04 records are reused *only* to supplement retrospective individual numeric context, and are marked historical DRAFT not reaudited.
- **99 of 150** candidates lack PL starts from at least one of the previous two seasons. This can mean the player played outside the eligible PL team pool or overseas, and must not imply no football participation.
- **150 of 150** players still need full match-by-match verification of most frequently deployed specific field position, particularly flexible LM/RM, fullbacks and withdrawn forwards.
- Club's actual highest performance is not automatically credited as a personal grade. PFA and Opta contemporary peer assessment are *supporting evidence*, not the sole grading basis.
- Important personnel events: partial-season Everton loan Mikel Arteta, winter departure Thomas Gravesen, early Newcastle-to-Celtic loan for Craig Bellamy, 2004-05 heavy injuries for Sol Campbell / Ruud van Nistelrooy, and summer recruits Robben / Carvalho / Ferreira / Drogba. Their PL-only appearances rather than subsequent reputations control ratings.
- A stat count entered in JSON is not an audited independent two-source confirmation. Birthdate/individual role/goals and historic supporting evidence remain open to a source-by-source check.
- Structural consistency checks (slot caps, 8×11 nominal starters, DOB format, identical XI ID links and position rank sequence) are separate from independently executed Node CLI and site browser checks; never imply they are equivalent.
- Local Node CLI checks **not run**. Deployed GitHub Pages 5-view interaction / crest display **not visually browser verified**. Final user approval **not granted**.
