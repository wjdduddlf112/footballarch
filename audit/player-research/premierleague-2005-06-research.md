## B. Formation research, why the 4-4-2 keeps recurring

The requested league formation is not a modern assumption. Arsenal mostly used 4-4-2 **in the Premier League** but 4-5-1 in the Champions League. Tottenham and West Ham have archived league match lineups in 4-4-2. Chelsea used 4-3-3 as their actual club structure. The UEFA 2005-06 Technical Report (p. 16) found 4-3-3/4-5-1 and 4-4-2 nearly even among the top 16 *European* clubs; that European sample cannot establish a numerical PL-wide frequency. Thus 4-4-2 is the **proposed**, not statistically conclusively verified, PL representative. The team-specific formations remain separately stored.

Caution: Match-by-match primary-position tallies are incomplete. The strongly supported exception is Steven Gerrard's 2005-06 majority **right-sided midfield**, based on contemporary manager Rafa Benítez's later specific statement that he played primarily on the right in 2005-06, scoring 23 goals across all competitions. He is assigned RW, not today's typical CM label. Club template uses LM/RM→LW/RW for this era.
