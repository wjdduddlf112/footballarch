## Historical verification and evaluation limitations

- Every evaluation club has 11 nominated starters, plus up to one bench candidate per position. The nominations with `appearances: null` require direct 2017-18 continental match verification.
- Only the UEFA technical observer selection and a few additional UEFA-sourced players have provisional individual tiers; a full 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18 recency-weighted investigation of every candidate is not complete.
- Every individual position ranking shown here is only within the currently graded subset. Full positional rankings require rating and validating all candidates.
- Birth date `—` means unidentified, not estimated; unverified appearances, starts, minutes, goals and assists are null, never silently zero.
- All tiers remain `draft_unapproved` and Best XIs `proposed_unapproved`.
- UEFA 2017–18 technical report states 556 UEL main-stage goals, although the current UEFA stats landing page shows 555; fixture score sum is 556.
