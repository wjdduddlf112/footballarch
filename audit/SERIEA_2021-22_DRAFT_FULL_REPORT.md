## Method and evidence limits

- Season 2021-22 is graded with 2019-20 then 2020-21 as less heavily weighted supporting periods; the target season has greatest emphasis. Trophy count and club league finishing position are not automatic player-grade bonuses.
- Qualifying scope: 2021-22 Serie A clubs that **actually appeared in UEFA 2022-23**. Four UCL, two UEL, one UECL. Atalanta 8th is excluded.
- Current-year appearances, starts, minutes, goals were manually transcribed from the seven linked BDFutbol 2021-22 domestic Serie A lineup tables. All 133 nominations played in the target domestic season. Goalkeeper G columns in BDFutbol are **goals conceded**, never scored; stored goals=0.
- Full independent confirmation of each player’s individual season line, exact DOB and main-position match-frequency remains **pending**. Likewise older years in prior Archive DRAFT do not constitute independent corroboration.
- Prior-year player-year numeric slots linked from unapproved Archive DRAFT: **149** / 266; **117** are not linked, recorded null. No zero-filled missing historical appearances and no fabricated assist totals.
- Every individual tier/rank is provisional, with tier rationale. Same-tier ranking is a qualitative current-performance proposal with prior-year context incomplete, not a measured or fixed numerical model.
- Two eligible club-spell records refer to **one person, Dušan Vlahović**: Fiorentina 21 Serie A apps, 17 G; Juventus 15 apps, 7 G. Do not combine team-specific counts; he appears only once at most in a league XI.
- Formation selections are historically plausible **representative** tactical descriptions, not verified league-wide mode. Inter left/right wing-backs map to the website LB/RB positions while `originalTacticalRole` maintains LWB/RWB.
