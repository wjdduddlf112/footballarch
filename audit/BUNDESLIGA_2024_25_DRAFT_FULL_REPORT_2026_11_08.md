## Individual assessment method
1. 2024-25 current individual league participation, main tactical role, attacking and defending influence weighted highest.
2. 2023-24 secondary, 2022-23 tertiary; prior-year from unapproved archive only as partial pointer, **never assumed independent verified**.
3. Player contribution and substitutability, not club success or trophy bonuses.
4. Rates are qualitative *draft* judgments, not match-by-match scored historical proof. Same-tier rank has starts/minutes provisional tiebreak.
5. 2025-26 and later player performances not retroactively applied.
6. A0 tier calibration uses 2024-25 contemporary DFL/VDV Team of the Season and Opta review in supporting context. It does not fill gaps in previous seasons.

## Historical and structural validation
- 18 unique Bundesliga table rows, seven confirmed next-season European entrants (four UCL, two UEL, one UECL including Cup winner Stuttgart).
- Every player: unique ID, one primary position, 2024-25 league appearances 1..34 and valid starts/minutes, season 2024-25, DOB YYYY-MM-DD and both Korean/original name.
- At most one backup per club per position. Multiple starter-equivalent phases allowed with source-grounded actual use. No requirement to fill unrepresented slots: Mainz has no LW/RW.
- Gap-free ranks for each position; XI has 11 unique players, matching 4-2-3-1 slots and selected player positions.
- S/A+/A/B... stored A for display A0, similarly B0/C0, compatible with frontend.
- External 2024-25 team roster sources and DFB DOB individually linked, but detailed matchwise roles / DOB second source / historical three-season player performance **not complete**.
- Live homepage browser visual inspection has not been performed in this environment. A GitHub Pages deploy success is not a human rendered-image check.
