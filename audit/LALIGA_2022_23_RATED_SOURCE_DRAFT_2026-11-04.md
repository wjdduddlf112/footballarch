## 2. 평가/전술/근거 적용 규칙
- **Three-season quality weighting**: 2020-21 weakest, 2021-22 medium, 2022-23 strongest, conceptually ~10/30/60. The third-season individual form comparison has NOT been independently finished, so no fabricated weighted player score. Current league PM, SM and goals documented from BDFutbol one-source club season tables.
- **Team trophy and league final position are criteria for which teams to study, not a numerical bonus to a player's tier.** Quality, consistency, individual contribution, actual starting/playing time and same-position comparison prioritized.
- **4-4-2 representative Best XI is PROVISIONAL**. Exact 2022-23 La Liga match-by-match modal formation not established; adjacent years' tactical trends are not proof of this individual season. Distinguish individual club formations (Real/Barça 4-3-3, Betis 4-2-3-1, Sociedad diamond 4-3-1-2, Atlético wingbacks etc).
- BDFutbol PM = league appearances; SM = league starts; G = outfield goals. Goalkeeper G on BDFutbol is negative for conceded goals, coded as zero *player scored* goals. Manual current-year transcription from one source per club, independent second-source checks remain OPEN.
- Single representative position per player, left and right differentiated, not a certified match-by-match majority position for all 151. Each of 8 clubs has exactly 11 nominal starters and at most one positional backup for each role; CB has two starters + one alternate, CM 2+1, ST 2+1 in two-forward squads.
- Display S, A+, A0, A-, B+, B0, B-, C+, C0, C-. JSON normalization `A/B/C` maps to `A0/B0/C0`; World Class=S or A+, League Top Class=S/A+/A0/A- including WC.
