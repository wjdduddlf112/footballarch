## 1. Scope, qualification and method

- Evaluate **2021-22 → 2022-23 → 2023-24**, the newest season with highest weight. Team title/finish itself is not individual-player grade bonus.
- Only 2023-24 Bundesliga clubs that **actually entered 2024-25 UEFA competitions**, including UECL qualifiers; source standings from RSSSF and UEFA/Bundesliga official participants.
- Freshly selected 2023-24 Bundesliga player club-season nominations; do not import prior Football Archive nominee pools.
- 2023-24 league apps/starts/minutes/goals sourced from period BDFutbol club tables, person-row secondary check pending. DOB reference linked to official DFB 2023-24 team roster; individual independent DOB validation pending.
- Previous 2021-22/2022-23 data are mostly matched prior **UNAPPROVED FOOTBALL ARCHIVE DRAFT** records, not independently historically verified; outside-league players may have missing records.
- Four specific Tottenham/Arsenal prior-year records have new independent league source references, but **zero entire three-season player qualitative audits** completed.
- Stats unknown = `null`, not zero. GK negative BDF goal column is goals conceded, never scored. Assists stored with **DFB-specific** attribution; other assist values remain null.
- Positions mapped as GK CB LB RB DM CM CAM LW RW ST. Left/right distinction held. Club 3-back LWB/RWB mapped to site LB/RB **with original tactical role separately recorded**. No invented empty position nominations.
- 2023-24 league representative 4-2-3-1 **provisional**, without independently counting most-used formation in all 306 games; club-specific shapes recorded separately.
- Unlimited actual seasonal starter-equivalents, at most one backup per club-position. Role based on league starts/minutes and season rotation; further matchwise review pending.
